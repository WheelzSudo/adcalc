Key Advertising Profitability Formulas

Use these core formulas to calculate maximum allowable customer acquisition costs (CPA) and break-even points:

Gross Profit Margin Per Order = AOV - COGS

Break-Even ROAS = AOV ÷ (AOV - COGS)

Maximum Target CPA (Breakeven) = AOV - COGS

Target Gross Revenue = Monthly Ad Budget × Target ROAS

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Break-Even ROAS calculated?

Break-Even ROAS is calculated by dividing Average Order Value (AOV) by gross margin per order: Break-Even ROAS = AOV ÷ (AOV - COGS). If your AOV is $100 and COGS is $40, your break-even ROAS is 1.67x ($100 / $60).

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) evaluates gross revenue per dollar spent on ad platforms (Revenue / Ad Spend). ROI (Return on Investment) calculates net profit percentage after deducting all operating expenses including product COGS, shipping, agency fees, and tech stack costs.

How does Average Order Value (AOV) impact ROAS targets?

Higher AOV provides greater dollar margins per transaction, lowering your required break-even ROAS and permitting higher bidding flexibility on ad networks to acquire customers competitively.
